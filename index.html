Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers before you get started.

Does requesting a quote enroll me in coverage?

No. The form only begins a conversation. You are not enrolled unless you separately choose a plan and complete the required enrollment process.

Can you help self-employed and 1099 workers?

Yes. Garrett works with contractors, consultants, entrepreneurs and other independent professionals.

Can you review coverage I already have?

Yes. A coverage review can help you understand your current plan and compare alternatives that may be available.

Can you help a business with employee benefits?

Yes. Options may be available for small businesses, growing teams and multi-location companies.

Is there a cost to speak with Garrett?

There is no cost or obligation to request a coverage review and compare options.
